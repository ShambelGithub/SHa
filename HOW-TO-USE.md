# How to Use This Thesis

## Guide for Different Audiences

This thesis on "Leveraging Public Procurement Policy for the Sustainability of Digital Startups" is designed to be accessible and useful to multiple audiences. This guide helps you navigate the content based on your role and interests.

---

## For Policymakers

### If you have 15 minutes:
Read:
1. **Abstract** (thesis.md) - Overview of the research
2. **Section 7.2: Core Recommendations** (thesis.md) - Specific policy recommendations
3. **Section 7.3: Policy Framework** (thesis.md) - Proposed framework structure

### If you have 1 hour:
Add:
1. **Section 1: Introduction** - Context and research problem
2. **Section 5.2: Policy Mechanisms** - Overview of different approaches
3. **Section 6.2: Policy Implications** - Design principles and implementation considerations
4. **Appendix B: Policy Templates** (appendices/policy-templates.md) - Practical examples

### If you have half a day:
Read the entire thesis focusing on:
- Section 5: Analysis and Findings (empirical evidence)
- Section 6: Discussion (implications and trade-offs)
- Section 7: Conclusions and Recommendations (comprehensive guidance)

### Key Takeaways:
- Public procurement can be a powerful tool for startup sustainability
- Success requires deliberate policy design AND quality implementation
- Balance innovation objectives with procurement integrity
- Learn from international examples while adapting to local context

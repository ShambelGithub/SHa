# Leveraging Public Procurement Policy for the Sustainability of Digital Startups

## About This Thesis

This repository contains the research thesis exploring how public procurement policies can be leveraged to support the sustainability and growth of digital startups. The study examines the intersection of government procurement practices, policy frameworks, and the digital startup ecosystem.

## Structure

- `thesis.md` - Main thesis document
- `references.md` - Bibliography and references
- `appendices/` - Supporting materials and data

## Abstract

Digital startups face significant challenges in achieving long-term sustainability, including limited access to markets, funding constraints, and regulatory barriers. This thesis investigates how public procurement policies can serve as a strategic tool to foster the growth and sustainability of digital startups. By analyzing case studies, policy frameworks, and empirical evidence, this research provides recommendations for policymakers and startup ecosystems.

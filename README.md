# Leveraging Public Procurement Policy for the Sustainability of Digital Startups

## Thesis Overview

This repository contains a comprehensive academic thesis examining how public procurement policy can be strategically leveraged to support the sustainability and growth of digital startups.

## Abstract

Public procurement represents a significant economic force, accounting for 12-20% of GDP in most developed economies. This thesis examines how procurement policy can be used as a tool to support digital startup sustainability while fostering innovation ecosystems. Through comparative analysis across multiple jurisdictions and comprehensive empirical research, the study identifies key barriers, evaluates policy interventions, and proposes evidence-based recommendations.

**Key Findings:**
- Digital startups face multi-dimensional barriers (procedural, financial, informational, cultural) in accessing procurement
- Well-designed policy interventions can significantly improve startup participation and sustainability
- Policy bundles combining multiple complementary interventions outperform single policies
- Different types of support are needed at different startup lifecycle stages
- Effective procurement policies create broader innovation ecosystem benefits
